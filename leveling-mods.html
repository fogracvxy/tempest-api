Leveling and skills

Leveling mods for server owners

What the Tempest plugins do with Endless Leveling and MMO Skill Tree, alone or together, and where it is set.

Tempest works with Endless Leveling (by Airijko) and MMO Skill Tree (by Ziggfreed). Nothing of either mod is shipped: Tempest Core finds them through their public APIs within half a minute of the server starting. Without them, nothing changes, and no level requirement ever locks a player out because a mod is missing.

#One mod, or both

InstalledWhat happens
Endless Leveling onlyIts level counts for everything below; its prestige, dungeons, wave gates, raids, classes and mob levels work too
MMO Skill Tree onlyIts skills become one level (total, combat, power level or the highest skill, as you choose); skill and achievement rewards work too
BothYou choose whose level counts ("Leveling mod" on the Leveling tab). Only that mod's level-ups pay level-up money, so nothing is paid twice. Endless Leveling's dungeons, gates and raids and MMO Skill Tree's skill and achievement rewards keep working. "Double leveling XP" raises both

#Where to set it: /tempest

Every amount starts at 0, which is off: installing a mod changes nothing until you choose.

TabShownWhat is on it
LevelingWith either modWhich mod counts (with "In use: ..."), money per level-up and milestones, the level bonus on other rewards, chat lines, boards, XP boosts
Leveling extrasWith either modCommands at levels, leveling XP from the economy (daily rewards, crates, contracts, the shop, town projects), levels for paying and gambling (stops alts), town bonuses
Endless LevelingOnly with Endless LevelingPrestige (money, a command, a bonus), dungeon, gate and raid money, mob gold by mob level, elite mobs
MMO Skill TreeOnly with MMO Skill TreeWhich number is "the level", which skill gets Tempest's XP, money and items per skill level and at milestones, achievement and server-first rewards
Pass leveling, PvP leveling, Events levelingWith their pluginPass XP for level-ups (and Endless Leveling clears), PvP rewards by level, events that start with raids (Endless Leveling)

#What players get

PluginWith a leveling mod
CoreMoney for level-ups, commands and items at levels, a bonus on other rewards by level, placeholders and a "Highest levels" board
QuestsGoals: reach a level; with Endless Leveling also prestige, dungeons, gates, raids and strong mobs; with MMO Skill Tree reach a skill level, gain skill XP, earn achievements, finish its quests, talk to characters. Rewards can give leveling XP (a goal about one skill gives that skill's XP)
Shop, Crates, QuestsItems, crates and quests that need a level (and with Endless Leveling a class)
PassPass XP for level-ups; tiers can give leveling XP
JobsJob work gives leveling XP; a job can need a level
ContractsPvP rewards for underdogs and fines for bullies, by level
Events"Double leveling XP" events; with Endless Leveling community goals for dungeons, gates and raids

#MMO Skill Tree rewards

RewardHow it works
Money per skill levelPaid for every level gained in a skill, more at higher levels; only above the highest level ever paid, so a reset pays nothing again
Skill milestonesA bonus, items (what does not fit goes to /mail) and a line for everyone every so many levels
Only these skillsLimit the rewards to the skills you list
AchievementsMoney per achievement and per achievement point, once each
Server firstsA bonus, items and a line for everyone for an achievement only the first player can earn

Placeholders: {leveling_level} (the level that counts), {mmo_total_level}, {mmo_combat_level}, {mmo_power}.

Note

MMO Skill Tree reads a player when they join, every half minute and after every XP gain. A player who has just joined may show no level for a moment.
