Wallets, coins, banks, daily rewards and ore and monster gold.

### Wallet and ATM


**Your wallet**

- Coins in your wallet are safe. Coins you carry as items are not: you can drop or lose them like anything else in your bags.
- Open your wallet with /wallet (or /bal), or at any ATM.

**Taking coins out**

- Each coin in the wallet has buttons to take out 1, 10 or 100 of it.

**Putting coins in**

- Press "Put all my coins in" to put every coin you carry into your wallet. You can also hold a coin and right-click.

**Paying someone**

- Press "Pay a player" in the wallet and pick someone who is online, or type /pay <player> <amount>.

**History**

- The bottom of the wallet shows your latest gains and spending.

**ATMs**

- An ATM opens the same wallet, and pays out with a shower of coins. ATMs are shown on your map.
- Only admins can build ATMs on this server.

**Admins also read:**


**Your rank perks: VIP**

- Daily rewards +100%
- Shop prices -15%
- Shop sell prices +10%
- Payment fee -100%
- Quest rewards +50%

### Earning coins


**Fighting**

- Creatures you defeat drop coins. The tougher the creature, the more it drops.

**Mining**

- Ores sometimes drop coins when you mine them. Rarer ores drop more:
- Copper Ore: 10% chance of 1 copper
- Iron Ore: 10% chance of 2 copper
- Silver Ore: 15% chance of 5 copper
- Gold Ore: 15% chance of 10 copper
- Cobalt Ore: 20% chance of 20 copper
- Mithril Ore: 25% chance of 50 copper
- Adamantite Ore: 30% chance of 100 copper
- Thorium Ore: 20% chance of 30 copper
- Onyxium Ore: 25% chance of 60 copper
- Ores that players placed give no coins.

**Every day**

- Claim a free reward once a day with /daily. Come back on following days for bigger rewards.

**Selling**

- Sell items you do not need at a shop stall. See the Shop page.

**Luck**

- Try the slot machines or the lottery for a big win, but over time luck costs more than it pays.

**Other players**

- Trade with other players and pay each other with /pay or the wallet.

### Daily rewards

- Your reward for day 1 is ready! Open the calendar to claim it.

**How it works**

- Claim once a day with /daily. Each day in a row moves you along the calendar to a bigger reward. Miss a day and you start again from day 1. After day 7 the calendar starts over.

**The rewards**

- Day 1: 50 copper
- Day 2: 60 copper
- Day 3: 70 copper
- Day 4: 80 copper
- Day 5: 100 copper
- Day 6: 120 copper
- Day 7: 250 copper

### Shared banks


**Saving up together**

- A shared bank holds coins for a group: a town, a team, friends saving for something big. Open one with /bank (it costs 500 copper) and add your friends by name.

**Who can do what**

- Everyone in the bank can put coins in. Only the owner, and members the owner allows, can take coins out. The history shows who put in and took out what.

**Closing**

- Members can leave whenever they like. When the owner closes the bank, whatever is in it goes to the owner.

**Your banks**

- Steves_Team: 1,000 copper

- Each board shows the top 10, and your own place under it.
- Open them with /leaderboards (or /ranks).

## Settings

Every setting is changed in game with `/tempest` and saved at once; nothing needs a restart. Amounts of 0 are off unless the setting says otherwise.

### Coins

`/tempest` → Coins. Who can change it: `tempest.admin` or `tempest.admin.coins`.

#### Earning

Coins players get from playing. Each ore's chance and worth are on the Ore Drops tab.

| Setting | What it does | Values |
|---|---|---|
| Mobs drop coins | When a player kills a mob. | On or off |
| Coins per point of health | Tougher mobs drop more. | 0 to 1000 |
| Most from one mob | 0 = no limit. | 0 to 1000000000 |
| Ores drop coins | Ores placed by players never do. | On or off |

#### Payments

Players paying each other with /pay or the wallet's Pay button.

| Setting | What it does | Values |
|---|---|---|
| Players can pay each other | Off: /pay and the wallet's Pay button stop working. Admins can still move money with /eco. | On or off |
| Smallest payment | The lowest amount a player can send with /pay. Stops players from spamming each other with tiny payments. | 1 to 1000000000 |
| Fee (%) | Paid on top by the sender. It leaves the game. | 0 to 100 |

#### Leaderboards

/leaderboards and /baltop.

| Setting | What it does | Values |
|---|---|---|
| Open to everyone | Off: only admins can see them. | On or off |
| Players on each board | How many players each leaderboard and /baltop lists, from first place down. | 1 to 50 |

#### Shared banks

Players open a bank with /bank and save up together; the owner decides who may take coins out.

| Setting | What it does | Values |
|---|---|---|
| Shared banks | Off: no new banks. Existing ones can still be used and closed. | On or off |
| Price to open a bank | It leaves the game. 0 = free. | 0 to 1000000000 |
| Banks one player can own | How many shared banks one player can open. Being a member of other players' banks does not count. | 1 to 100 |
| Members per bank | Besides the owner. | 1 to 200 |

#### Savings interest

Banks earn a little each day on the lowest balance they had since the last payout. A new bank earns from its second payout; a server that was down pays one day, not the days missed.

| Setting | What it does | Values |
|---|---|---|
| Savings interest | Off: no interest. New money: keep the rate low. | On or off |
| Rate (% a day) | What a bank earns each day, as a share of its lowest balance since the last payout. 1% on 10,000 is 100 a day. The limits below keep it from growing too fast. | 0 to 5 |
| Highest balance that earns | 0 = no limit. | 0 to 1000000000000 |
| Most per bank a day | 0 = no limit. | 0 to 1000000000 |
| Most per owner a day | All banks of one owner together, so many small banks earn no more. 0 = no limit. | 0 to 1000000000 |
| Payout hour | Server time. | 0 to 23 |

#### Other

#### ATM designs

Crouch and press F on an ATM to change its design, or use /atm <design> to get one to place: classic, vault, teller, royal, crystal. Every design works the same.

| Setting | What it does | Values |
|---|---|---|
| Players can craft ATMs | Off: only admins can. | On or off |
| Keep transaction logs (days) | 0 = forever. Old files are deleted when the server starts. | 0 to 36500 |

### Integrations

`/tempest` → Integrations. Who can change it: `tempest.admin` or `tempest.admin.coins`.

#### VaultUnlocked

Lets plugins that use VaultUnlocked (player shops, auction houses, jobs, land claims...) pay and charge in these coins. Install VaultUnlocked to use it.

| Setting | What it does | Values |
|---|---|---|
| Be the economy for VaultUnlocked plugins | Their payments show in wallets and the /eco log under the plugin's name. | On or off |

#### Cassaforte

Lets plugins that use Cassaforte (SimpleJobs, Towny3D...) pay and charge in these coins, and use the shared banks. Install Cassaforte to use it.

| Setting | What it does | Values |
|---|---|---|
| Be the economy for Cassaforte plugins | Cassaforte keeps the first economy that connects; switching off takes a restart. | On or off |

#### Discord

Posts big moments to a Discord channel. In Discord: Edit channel, Integrations, Webhooks, New webhook, Copy webhook URL, and paste it below.

| Setting | What it does | Values |
|---|---|---|
| Webhook link | Empty: Discord is off. Keep it secret, anyone with it can post. | Text |
| Name in Discord | The name the posts appear under in your Discord channel, such as your server's name. Empty: the name the webhook was given in Discord. | Text |
| Jackpots | Posts when a player wins a jackpot. | On or off |
| Big slot machine wins | The size is set on the Slot Machine tab (Announce wins from). | On or off |
| Lottery winners | Posts who won each lottery drawing and how much. | On or off |
| Achievements | Posts when a player unlocks an achievement or finishes a quest that announces itself, and when the server finishes a server quest together. | On or off |
| News from other mods | Posts the other big moments: bounties, crate key parties, events starting and ending, finished town projects and unlocks, and battle pass seasons and players who finish them. | On or off |
| Send a test message when saved | Tick, then Save: a test message is posted if the link works. | On or off |
