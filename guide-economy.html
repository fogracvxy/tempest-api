Economy (Coins)

Wallets, coins, banks, daily rewards and ore and monster gold.

#What players read in /guide

The /guide pages of this plugin, as a player sees them on a test server (numbers are examples).

#Wallet and ATM

Your wallet

  • Coins in your wallet are safe. Coins you carry as items are not: you can drop or lose them like anything else in your bags.
  • Open your wallet with /wallet (or /bal), or at any ATM.

Taking coins out

  • Each coin in the wallet has buttons to take out 1, 10 or 100 of it.

Putting coins in

  • Press "Put all my coins in" to put every coin you carry into your wallet. You can also hold a coin and right-click.

Paying someone

  • Press "Pay a player" in the wallet and pick someone who is online, or type /pay <player> <amount>.

History

  • The bottom of the wallet shows your latest gains and spending.

ATMs

  • An ATM opens the same wallet, and pays out with a shower of coins. ATMs are shown on your map.
  • Only admins can build ATMs on this server.

Admins also read:

Your rank perks: VIP

  • Daily rewards +100%
  • Shop prices -15%
  • Shop sell prices +10%
  • Payment fee -100%
  • Quest rewards +50%

#Earning coins

Fighting

  • Creatures you defeat drop coins. The tougher the creature, the more it drops.

Mining

  • Ores sometimes drop coins when you mine them. Rarer ores drop more:
  • Copper Ore: 10% chance of 1 copper
  • Iron Ore: 10% chance of 2 copper
  • Silver Ore: 15% chance of 5 copper
  • Gold Ore: 15% chance of 10 copper
  • Cobalt Ore: 20% chance of 20 copper
  • Mithril Ore: 25% chance of 50 copper
  • Adamantite Ore: 30% chance of 100 copper
  • Thorium Ore: 20% chance of 30 copper
  • Onyxium Ore: 25% chance of 60 copper
  • Ores that players placed give no coins.

Every day

  • Claim a free reward once a day with /daily. Come back on following days for bigger rewards.

Selling

  • Sell items you do not need at a shop stall. See the Shop page.

Luck

  • Try the slot machines or the lottery for a big win, but over time luck costs more than it pays.

Other players

  • Trade with other players and pay each other with /pay or the wallet.

#Daily rewards

  • Your reward for day 1 is ready! Open the calendar to claim it.

How it works

  • Claim once a day with /daily. Each day in a row moves you along the calendar to a bigger reward. Miss a day and you start again from day 1. After day 7 the calendar starts over.

The rewards

  • Day 1: 50 copper
  • Day 2: 60 copper
  • Day 3: 70 copper
  • Day 4: 80 copper
  • Day 5: 100 copper
  • Day 6: 120 copper
  • Day 7: 250 copper

#Shared banks

Saving up together

  • A shared bank holds coins for a group: a town, a team, friends saving for something big. Open one with /bank (it costs 500 copper) and add your friends by name.

Who can do what

  • Everyone in the bank can put coins in. Only the owner, and members the owner allows, can take coins out. The history shows who put in and took out what.

Closing

  • Members can leave whenever they like. When the owner closes the bank, whatever is in it goes to the owner.

Your banks

  • Steves_Team: 1,000 copper

#Leaderboards

  • See who leads the server: the richest players, the top earners, the biggest single wins, and the best at the slot machines, the lottery and the shop, as far as this server has them.
  • Each board shows the top 10, and your own place under it.
  • Open them with /leaderboards (or /ranks).

#All commands

Claims

  • /claims -Your party and its land

Coinflip

  • /coinflip (or /cf) -Open the rooms: join one, watch a flip, or make your own
  • /cf create <amount> [heads|tails] -Open a room with a stake (heads if you pick no side)
  • /cf join <player> -Join a player's room with the same stake
  • /cf cancel -Close your open rooms; your stakes come back
  • /cf list -List the open rooms in chat

Coins

  • /coins -This help screen
  • /wallet (or /bal) -Your wallet: withdraw, deposit, pay and history
  • /pay <player> <amount> -Give money to another player
  • /daily -The daily reward calendar
  • /baltop (or /rich) -The richest players, in chat
  • /bank -Your shared bank accounts: save up together with friends
  • /leaderboards (or /ranks) -Leaderboards: richest, top earners, biggest wins and more

Contracts

  • /contracts -Open the contracts board

General

  • /guide -This help screen
  • /hud -Show or hide your money counter; /hud top, left or low moves it
  • /board -Pick what your board on the right of the screen shows, or hide it
  • /mail -Your mailbox: rewards that did not fit or came while you were away
  • /info (or /rules, /serverinfo) -The server's rules and info, from the admins
  • /places -Places on the world map, nearest first; pick what your map shows

Crates

  • /crates -Open crates for prizes
  • /cratekey <player> <bronze|silver|gold> <amount> -Give crate keys (admins)

  • /events -What is on, what is coming, and the community goal

Jobs

  • /jobs -The jobs and your levels

Lottery

  • /lottery (or /lotto) -The current round: pot, tickets and time left
  • /lottery buy <amount> -Buy tickets for the current round

Battle pass

  • /pass -Your tiers, rewards and XP this season

Town projects

  • /projects -See the projects and donate

Quests

  • /quests -Your quests and the quest board
  • /achievements (or /ach) -Your achievements and how close you are

Shop

  • /shop -Open the shop (where the server allows it)
  • /sell hand -Sell the item in your hand (where the server allows /shop)
  • /sell all -Sell everything the shop buys (where the server allows /shop)

Slot Machine

Town

Trade

  • /trade -Trade with a player

#Savings interest

Banks earn a little each day on the lowest balance they had since the last payout. A new bank earns from its second payout; a server that was down pays one day, not the days missed.

SettingWhat it doesValues

Rate (% a day)What a bank earns each day, as a share of its lowest balance since the last payout. 1% on 10,000 is 100 a day. The limits below keep it from growing too fast.0 to 5
Highest balance that earns0 = no limit.0 to 1000000000000
Most per bank a day0 = no limit.0 to 1000000000
Most per owner a dayAll banks of one owner together, so many small banks earn no more. 0 = no limit.0 to 1000000000
