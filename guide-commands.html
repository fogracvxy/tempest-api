Server guide

All commands and permissions

Every command of every Tempest plugin, and who can use it.

Every command of the Tempest plugins, with the permission it needs. "Everyone" works for every player; a permission means only players or groups given it.

#Core

CommandWho can use itWhat it does
/board (/sidebar)EveryonePick what your board on the right of the screen shows, or hide it
/boostertempest.boostersGive a player a booster for a while (admins, crate and vote rewards)
/guide (/serverhelp)EveryoneHow the server's shop, crates, quests and money work
/hud (/coinhud)EveryoneShow or hide your money counter (/hud top, left or low moves it)
/hud leftEveryoneMoney counter in the top left corner
/hud lowEveryoneMoney counter on the right side, lower down
/hud topEveryoneMoney counter in a row along the top, right of centre
/mail (/mailbox)EveryoneOpen your mailbox: rewards and items kept for you
/placesEveryonePlaces on the world map, and what your map shows
/places addtempest.admin.mapAdds a place to the map where you stand (admins)
/serverinfo (/rules, /info)EveryoneThe server's rules and info
/tempest (/casino)EveryoneTempest: the settings menu for admins, the help screen for players
/tempest doctortempest.adminFinds ways players could make money from nothing in the settings (admins)
/tempest infotempest.adminTempest plugins: versions, editions and the economy (admins)
/tempest logtempest.adminWho changed what: the newest lines of the admin log (admins)
/tempest reporttempest.adminWrites a support report to paste when asking for help (admins)
/tempest settingsEveryoneOpen the Tempest settings menu (admins)
/tempest textsEveryoneChange any text players see (admins)

/coins (/coinhelp)EveryoneHelp for coins, the wallet, banks and daily rewards
/dailyEveryoneOpen the daily reward calendar
/economy (/eco)coins.admin.economyAdmin: the economy screen, and changing balances
/economy addcoins.admin.economyGive a player money

/economy setcoins.admin.economySet a player's balance to an amount
/economy takecoins.admin.economyTake money from a player
/leaderboards (/leaderboard, /ranks)EveryoneThe richest players, biggest winners and best traders

/wallet (/balance, /bal)EveryoneOpen your wallet: balance, coins, payments and history
