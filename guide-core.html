#What players read in /guide

The /guide pages of this plugin, as a player sees them on a test server (numbers are examples).

#Money counter

  • The number in the corner of your screen is your balance in copper. When it changes, the gain or loss flashes beside it in green or red.
  • Next to it you may see short notes: a daily reward waiting, the slot machine jackpot, the lottery pot, a quest you are tracking, or what the shop pays for the item in your hand.

Your counter

  • It is shown, in the "top" position.
  • /hud hides or shows it. /hud top, /hud left and /hud low move it.

#Items from other mods

Enchanted (Simple Enchantments), reforged (Socket Reforge) and any mod's items keep what they have in shops, crates, the pass, quests, jobs, events, mail, trade and the market. Hold one and type /tempest item to see every field it has and how it is treated. Names below are field names; * is anything (*.XP fits SocketReforge.Lore.Socket.XP).

SettingWhat it doesValues
Fields that change with useCommas between, * for anything: .XP, *KillCount Shown to players, but never used to tell two items apart: experience, kill counts and the like. Without this a sword that gained experience would no longer sell back to its shop line.Text
More fields to hideCommas between, * for anything A mod's own bookkeeping, neither shown nor matched. Tooltips, hashes and versions are hidden already.Text
Fields that hold other itemsCommas between, * for anything An item with something in one of these is never copied into a shop, a prize or a reward (its contents would be copied too). Backpacks and containers the usual way are found already.Text
Fields that make an item one of a kindCommas between, * for anything An item with one of these is never copied (a bound item, a named pet); it can still be traded. Ids written as UUIDs are found already.Text
Use the name a mod gave the itemA reforged "Iron Sword +3" shows by that name in shops, lists and mail. Off: the game's name.On or off
