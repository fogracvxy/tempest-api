Switching to Tempest

Move your server to Tempest without losing anyone's money, and what works next to the plugins you already run. Every line on this page was tested on real servers.

Tempest is made to join a server that already has players. You can keep the economy you have (the Tempest plugins pay through it), or move every balance into Tempest Economy in one command. Either way, nothing is lost.

#Two ways to switch

Keep your economyMove to Tempest Economy
What you installTempest Core, the Tempest plugins you want, and VaultUnlockedTempest Core, Tempest Economy (Coins), the Tempest plugins you want
Players' moneyStays where it is; every Tempest plugin pays and charges in itCopied into Tempest Economy with /eco import
What you getCrates, quests, the pass, jobs, shops, events... on your moneyAll of that, plus physical coins, wallets, banks, ATMs, daily rewards, mob and ore gold and the /eco dashboard

Keeping your economy is explained on Economies. The rest of this page is about moving.

#Moving every balance: /eco import

  1. Keep your old economy installed. Add Tempest Core, Tempest Economy and VaultUnlocked, and start the server.
  2. Type /eco import list: it names every economy it can copy from.
  3. Type /eco import <name>. It shows how many players it found and how much money they have, and changes nothing.
  4. Type the same command again within a minute to copy. Every player's Tempest balance becomes their balance in the old economy, rounded down to whole coins. The wallets are backed up first, and nothing is taken from the old economy, so you can check before you remove it.
  5. Remove the old economy and restart.

Some economies do not tell other plugins who has an account. Tempest Economy then asks them about every player who has ever joined your server, so nobody is missed. A player who never joined your server is not known to it and is not copied.

Copying from YAS Currency, EliteEssentials, EconomyAPI and Coins & Markets needs Tempest Economy newer than 2.7.2 (the next release). Ecotale, PJ Money and EssentialsPlus work with 2.7.2 already.

#Moving from Coins & Markets

Coins & Markets keeps its money in coins players carry, in their inventory and Coin Pouch, and only sees them while the player is online. So it is exchanged instead of copied:

  1. Ask players to withdraw their vendor earnings and take coins out of chests, into their pouch.
  2. Type /eco import CoinsAndMarkets. It shows the rate and how many players are online.
  3. Type it again within a minute to start. Everyone online is exchanged at once: their Coins & Markets coins are taken and the same value is paid into their wallet, and they are told. Everyone else is exchanged when they next join.
  4. Keep Coins & Markets installed until everyone has been on, then type /eco import stop and remove it.

The rate is 1 copper to 1 coin. To choose another, start with /eco import CoinsAndMarkets --rate 100 (100 copper make one coin); copper that does not make a whole coin stays with the player. Coins are taken before anything is paid, and given back if paying fails. Every exchange is in the /eco transaction log.

#Questions

Can I try Tempest before switching? Yes. Install Tempest Core and the plugins you want next to your economy: they use it. Switch to Tempest Economy later, or never.

What if something goes wrong? /eco import backs the wallets up before it copies (the message says where), and the old economy keeps its balances until you remove it. /tempest backup makes a full backup of every Tempest plugin.

My economy is not in the list. If it works with VaultUnlocked, /eco import list shows it and the import works the same way. Tell us on Discord and we will test it.

Does the switch pay achievements? Tempest Quests counts money however it arrives, so an achievement such as "Rich" can be earned right after a switch, as it would be for any money.
