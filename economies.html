Economies for server owners

Which economy the Tempest plugins pay into: Tempest Economy, or one you already run through VaultUnlocked. Tested with Ecotale, YAS Currency, PJ Money, EssentialsPlus, EliteEssentials and GlymeraMerchant.

Every Tempest plugin pays and charges through Tempest Core, never through an economy directly. Core uses the first of these that runs:

OrderEconomyWhat you need
1Tempest Economy (Coins)Its jar in mods. Wallets, coin items, banks (/bank), ATMs, daily rewards, mob and ore gold
2Any economy through VaultUnlockedVaultUnlocked in mods, next to an economy mod that registers with it
noneNo economyThe plugins run; prices, paid rewards, taxes and upkeep simply wait

/tempest → General shows which one is in use ("Coins", "VaultUnlocked: GlymeraMerchant"...).

#Using the economy you already have

Install VaultUnlocked and your economy mod, and leave Tempest Economy (Coins) out: with Coins installed, Coins is the economy (and it registers itself with VaultUnlocked for other mods).

Economy modWorksNotes
Ecotale 1.2.1Yes, testedThrough VaultUnlocked. Switching to Coins later: /eco import Ecotale copies every balance
YAS Currency 1.95Yes, testedThrough VaultUnlocked
PJ Money 1.3.2Yes, testedThrough VaultUnlocked. Needs PJ-CoreLib, as always
EssentialsPlus 1.21.0Yes, testedIts economy is on by default and registers with VaultUnlocked
EliteEssentials 2.0.12Yes, testedTurn its economy on first: "economy": { "enabled": true } in mods/EliteEssentials/config.json (off by default)
GlymeraMerchantYes, tested in gameRegisters with VaultUnlocked by itself. Jobs pay, shop prices, crate keys, quest rewards, claim prices and party taxes all use its gold
Coins & Markets 3.0.4NoIts coins are not shared with other plugins (it has no VaultUnlocked economy). It runs fine next to Tempest Economy
Any other VaultUnlocked economyYesIt must register an economy with VaultUnlocked

"Tested" means on real servers with test players, 2026-11-22: the shop paid for sales and charged for purchases, a sale the economy refused gave the items back, quest and event rewards, the premium pass, slot bets, crates and coinflips were paid, and every coin added up at the end.

What changes without Tempest Economy: the money counter on the board, coin items, /bank, ATMs, daily rewards and mob and ore gold are part of Tempest Economy, so they are not there; your economy mod shows its own balance. A Tempest Claims party keeps its money inside Claims instead of a /bank account.

Amounts: Tempest works in whole coins. An economy with decimals is asked for whole amounts; what it reports is rounded down.

#Other plugins next to Tempest Economy

Run one economy per server. With two (Coins and another one on VaultUnlocked), other authors' plugins may pay into either. Tempest always uses Coins when it is installed.

PluginNext to Tempest Economy (Coins)What to set
Ecotale, YAS Currency, PJ MoneyWorks, testedNothing. To move to Coins: /eco import <economy> copies the balances, then remove the old one
EssentialsPlusWorks, tested, after one settingIts /pay, /money and /eco take over Tempest Economy's. In mods/fof1092_EssentialsPlus/config.json: "disabledCommands": ["/pay", "/money", "/eco"] and "economy": {"enabled": false}
EliteEssentialsWorks, testedNothing while its economy is off (the default). With it on, its /pay takes over: in mods/EliteEssentials/config.json set "useExternalEconomy": true and "vaultUnlockedProvider": false under "economy", and its commands use Tempest Economy's money
Coins & MarketsWorks side by side, testedNothing. Its coins and vendors stay its own
EconomyAPIWorks, tested, after one settingBy default it keeps a money of its own ($500 to start, paid for kills). In mods/Sennecoolgames_EconomyAPI/config.json set "bridgeMode": "VaultUnlocked", "disableMobKillRewards": true and "disablePlayerKillRewards": true: it then uses Tempest Economy's money
Ecotale MarketplaceWorks next to Ecotale, testedIt needs Ecotale and pays in Ecotale's money
LuckPerms, BetterMapRun side by side, testedNothing

#Switching to Tempest Economy

Keep your old economy installed next to Tempest Economy and VaultUnlocked, then type /eco import <economy>: it shows how many players and how much money it found. Type the same command again within a minute to copy. Each player's balance becomes their balance in the old economy, rounded down to whole coins. The wallets are backed up first and nothing is taken from the old economy. Then remove the old one.

Switching fromTypeTested
Ecotale/eco import EcotaleYes, every balance copied exactly
YAS Currency/eco import YASCurrencyYes
PJ Money/eco import PJ-MoneyYes
EssentialsPlus/eco import EssentialsPlusYes
EliteEssentials/eco import EliteEssentialsYes
EconomyAPI/eco import EconomyAPIYes
Coins & Markets/eco import CoinsAndMarketsYes: its coins are exchanged (below)
Any other economy on VaultUnlocked/eco import list shows its nameShould work: every player who ever joined your server is looked up

Players who never joined your server are not known to it, so their balances are not copied.

Coins & Markets keeps its money in coins players carry, so it is exchanged instead: after /eco import CoinsAndMarkets (and the same again to confirm), each player's Coins & Markets coins, in their inventory and pouch, are swapped for Tempest coins: everyone online at once, everyone else when they next join, until you type /eco import stop. 1 copper is 1 coin; /eco import CoinsAndMarkets --rate 100 makes 100 copper one coin. Coins in chests and vendor earnings are not reached, so tell players to withdraw them first, and keep Coins & Markets installed until everyone has been on.

#For economy mod authors

Register your economy with VaultUnlocked (Economy from the Vault2 API) and every Tempest plugin uses it, with no work on either side. If you would rather link directly, Tempest Core takes one economy:

java
Economies.register(myEconomy);     // an implementation of com.atomictempest.core.economy.Economy

It is preferred over VaultUnlocked; call Economies.unregister when your plugin stops. See Money.
