Economies for server owners

Which economy the Tempest plugins pay into: Tempest Economy, or one you already run through VaultUnlocked (GlymeraMerchant, Ecotale and others).

Every Tempest plugin pays and charges through Tempest Core, never through an economy directly. Core uses the first of these that runs:

OrderEconomyWhat you need
1Tempest Economy (Coins)Its jar in mods. Wallets, coin items, banks (/bank), ATMs, daily rewards, mob and ore gold
2Any economy through VaultUnlockedVaultUnlocked in mods, next to an economy mod that registers with it
noneNo economyThe plugins run; prices, paid rewards, taxes and upkeep simply wait

/tempest → General shows which one is in use ("Coins", "VaultUnlocked: GlymeraMerchant"...).

#Using the economy you already have

Install VaultUnlocked and your economy mod, and leave Tempest Economy (Coins) out: with Coins installed, Coins is the economy (and it registers itself with VaultUnlocked for other mods).

Economy modWorksNotes
GlymeraMerchantYes, tested in gameRegisters with VaultUnlocked by itself. Jobs pay, shop prices, crate keys, quest rewards, claim prices and party taxes all use its gold
EcotaleYesThrough VaultUnlocked
Any other VaultUnlocked economyYesIt must register an economy with VaultUnlocked

What changes without Tempest Economy: the money counter on the board, coin items, /bank, ATMs, daily rewards and mob and ore gold are part of Tempest Economy, so they are not there; your economy mod shows its own balance. A Tempest Claims party keeps its money inside Claims instead of a /bank account.

Amounts: Tempest works in whole coins. An economy with decimals is asked for whole amounts; what it reports is rounded down.

#For economy mod authors

Register your economy with VaultUnlocked (Economy from the Vault2 API) and every Tempest plugin uses it, with no work on either side. If you would rather link directly, Tempest Core takes one economy:

java
Economies.register(myEconomy);     // an implementation of com.atomictempest.core.economy.Economy

It is preferred over VaultUnlocked; call Economies.unregister when your plugin stops. See Money.
