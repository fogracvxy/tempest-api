Town

Features the town unlocks, and the mayor.

#What players read in /guide

The /guide pages of this plugin, as a player sees them on a test server (numbers are examples).

#Town

  • The whole server is one town, and it unlocks new features as it grows. /town shows what is unlocked and what is still needed.

How things unlock

  • Some unlocks wait for a town building to be finished: donate to it with /projects. Others come on a date or after another unlock.

#Commands

CommandWho can use itWhat it does
/mayorEveryoneTown: who the mayor is and how to become mayor
/mayor boostEveryoneMayor: give everyone a boost for a while
/mayor clearEveryoneAdmins: the town has no mayor until the term ends
/mayor fineEveryoneMayor: fine a player who misbehaved
/mayor rewardEveryoneMayor: reward a player who helped the town
/mayor setEveryoneAdmins: make an online player mayor until the term ends
/townEveryoneTown: what the town has unlocked and what it still needs
/town lockEveryoneAdmins: keep an unlock locked
/town unlockEveryoneAdmins: keep an unlock unlocked

#Settings

Every setting is changed in game with /tempest and saved at once; nothing needs a restart. Amounts of 0 are off unless the setting says otherwise.

#Town

/tempest → Town. Who can change it: tempest.admin or tempest.admin.town.

The town unlocks features as it grows. Make and change unlocks in /town, MANAGE tab; this tab is for all of them.

SettingWhat it doesValues
Town decides what is unlockedOff: every feature is on, as if Town were not installed. Unlocks keep their state for when it is on again.On or off
Players see locked unlocksOn: /town shows what is still locked and what it needs. Off: only what the town has.On or off

#Mayor

Each term the player who gave most to town projects becomes mayor, with a few small powers you limit here. Only in-game donations count. Players see it all with /mayor; admins can /mayor set or /mayor clear.

SettingWhat it doesValues
The town has a mayorOn: at the end of each term, the player who gave most to town projects in that term becomes mayor for the next one. Off: no mayor and no mayor powers.On or off
A term lasts (days)How long one mayor's term is. Donations are counted again from 0 for each term.1 to 90
Least given to become mayorIn points: one per coin, and the next setting per item.0 to 10000000
Points per item givenPoints for each item a player gives to a project. Each coin given is one point.0 to 10000
The mayor's boostFor everyone online, started with /mayor boost.Jobs pay, Mob coins, Ore coins, Quest reward, Shop sell, Daily reward, Pass xp, Shop discount, Contract pay
Boost (%)How much the mayor's boost raises what it boosts.1 to 100
For (minutes)How long one mayor's boost lasts.1 to 1440
Boosts each termHow many boosts the mayor can start in one term. 0: the mayor cannot start boosts.0 to 20
Reward (coins)/mayor reward <player>: new coins for a player who helped. Never the mayor, each player once a term.0 to 1000000
Rewards each termHow many players the mayor can reward in one term. 0: no rewards.0 to 20
Fine (coins)/mayor fine <player>: never more than they have, never an admin; only the two of them are told. Every use is in the admin log.0 to 1000000
